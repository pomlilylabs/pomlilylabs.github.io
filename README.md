## How an Engagement Works

1. **Discovery** — a short call or brief to pin down the outcome, the audience, and the constraints.
2. **Scope & quote** — a written scope with a fixed deliverable list and timeline before any work begins.
3. **Build** — staged delivery with check-ins at agreed points, so there are no surprises at the end.
4. **Delivery & support** — final files handed over in production-ready form, with a window for revisions and questions.
